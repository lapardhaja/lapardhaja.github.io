About

I’m an AI Engineer at the U.S. Department of the Treasury and a licensed Professional Engineer (P.E.) in New Jersey.

At Treasury, I build AI-enabled systems and data workflows that help modernize financial management. The goal is practical: reduce manual work and avoidable costs, make long-standing processes and legacy systems easier to use, and improve service for taxpayers. I use AI where it makes a task faster, clearer, or more dependable, and pair it with clear interfaces, testing, and documentation.

Before moving into AI engineering, I worked in transportation engineering and research. My work included traffic operations, simulation models, and large datasets—experience that taught me how to break down complicated systems and explain the result clearly.

I earned both a Ph.D. and an M.S. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from UC Berkeley. My doctoral research examined how adaptive cruise control affects traffic flow and urban congestion, combining field data, simulation, and predictive modeling. I also earned a Diploma of Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens, where I studied transportation and surveying engineering.

I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Analytics at Georgia Tech, focused on data science and machine learning. It gives me room to deepen the skills I use alongside my engineering background.
